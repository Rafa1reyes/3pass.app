The problem

Every company keeps its own list. That’s the problem.

Trespass information lives in binders, group texts, and officers’ memories. The moment a subject crosses the street to a property you don’t patrol, all that documentation stops mattering.

🗂️

Scattered records

Paper logs and photos in five different phones don’t hold up — and don’t transfer between shifts, let alone companies.

🕶️

Officers walking in blind

Your new hire has no idea the person at the loading dock was trespassed twice last month by the company across town.

⚖️

Documentation that folds

When police or property managers ask for the paper trail, “Dave remembers him” isn’t documentation.

The fix

Everything your officers need, in the pocket they already have

📑

Patrol reports that write themselves

Tap a property, pick the report type, write the disposition, attach photos. Date, time, officer name and badge stamp themselves on submit. Filed into a folder per property, sortable by officer, type, or date.

📟

Dispatch to the officer’s phone

Log a service call, assign it, and it lands on that officer’s app with the address, the details, and one tap to navigate. They mark themselves en route, file the report, and the call closes itself.

📄

Reports your customer can read

Every report exports as a clean PDF on your letterhead and goes straight to the property manager — with a record of exactly what was sent and when.

📋

Shared subject registry

Photos, descriptions, incident history, and active 602 status — searchable in seconds, shared across every connected company in your region.

🗺️

Live property map

Every covered property pinned with its active trespasses. Officers see exactly what’s active where they’re standing.

📝

Clean 602 workflow

Document a trespass admonishment in under two minutes — subject, property, photos, expiration. Court-ready record every time.

🤝

Cross-company sharing

Companies choose what to share with the regional network. Compete for contracts, cooperate on safety.

⏰

Expirations handled

602s expire automatically on schedule, so your registry stays accurate — and defensible — without spreadsheet babysitting.

👮

You decide who can do what

Flip permissions per officer — who files which reports, who dispatches, who sees the whole company’s work, who emails customers. Decide what’s mandatory on a report, or let your officers file with what they have.
